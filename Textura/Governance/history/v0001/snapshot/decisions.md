# Shared policies and configurable starter defaults

This document describes the sanitized 0.1.2 distribution. It is not a transcript, a private author's decision log or evidence of a new user's consent. Personal subscriptions, local paths, aesthetic preferences, private decisions and private record identifiers are excluded. Authors may override editorial defaults for a work; provenance, honest capability statements and applicable processing restrictions remain required.

## Shared capabilities

- Support author-led co-conception, drafting, discussion and revision across languages and genres.
- Organize persistent topics, multiple research objects, works and optional series without forcing a single outline or workflow.
- Keep sources in any relevant language. Distinguish discovery, acquisition, extraction, interpretation and support for a particular claim.
- Preserve immutable originals, exact versions, actual locators, honest capture limits and dissenting assessments.
- Use recognition, challenge and countercritique for review. Prefer isolated initial analyses, then cross-review. When isolation is unavailable, a disclosed sequential procedure is possible if suitable for the task; differing role labels never establish independence.
- Distinguish author decisions, AI proposals, observations and open questions. An accepted suggestion is not a permanent aesthetic preference.
- Preserve meaningful state and selected evidence; do not archive all dialogue by default.

## Starter editorial behavior

These are configurable starting choices, not claims about an author's preferences or universally required literary standards.

- Begin research with a small scoped search and discussion; deepen it in response to the task.
- Offer candidate edits with reasons and preserve the author's choice. Direct changes need actual task scope and version preservation.
- Treat works in different languages independently unless a translation/adaptation relationship is chosen.
- Prioritize natural expression and work quality. Provide learning explanations when useful without imposing a fixed learner proficiency ceiling.
- For free verse, preserve line/stanza form and an initial reading before supplying author-intent notes.
- Use numbered references with endnotes/sources as a prose starter convention; allow work- and medium-specific presentation while retaining internal traceability.
- Pair translated quotations with the inspected original, source, version and translation attribution. Body/notes presentation is adjustable.
- Label exploratory statements, counterexamples and gaps. Do not promote a hypothesis to an established premise through repetition.
- In argumentative writing, consider strong relevant objections and reasoned responses or remaining limitations. AI-generated objections are hypothetical unless attributed with evidence.
- Prepare an accurate short AI-assistance statement for public work as a starter convention; describe only actual contributions.
- Preserve prior versions. Prepare dated explanations for substantive public corrections; minor corrections without a meaning change may need no separate note.

## External actions and processing

New CLI organization and source records default to local-only processing in this candidate. A host may use cloud processing only within explicit authorization and the applicable policy; descendants retain restrictive source policies. Privacy labels guide behavior and are not encryption or an operating-system barrier.

Publishing produces prepared files and content. The author chooses any actual publication. Feedback uses selected reader comments, with no automatic collection or sending.

The optional Bibliotheca bridge is disabled and has no configured path. If deliberately configured, it provides limited-reference search/show/cite through the supported external CLI. Old notes and conversations are leads, not original proof or current beliefs. Selected preservation packets are proposals, not write operations.

No paid model integration, backup service, scheduler, automatic library write or account connection is enabled. Future capabilities need concrete implementation and explicit scope. Component histories and manuscript versions are not a separate backup.
