# Style and fiction module collaboration

Status: operating protocols and blank templates for release 0.1.2. This document does not add CLI verbs, model calls or automatic statistical analysis. Shared capabilities are described in [style-fiction-decisions.md](style-fiction-decisions.md); actual author choices belong to the user's records.

## Ownership and interfaces

| Owner | Inputs | Authoritative outputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Topics | Work/topic/series organization | Existing work, topic and series identities and explicit relations |
| Writing | Authored text, trials, exercises, candidate prose and adoption | Text versions and candidate edits; imported original bytes remain immutable |
| Style | Selected Writing samples or Sources/Reading references, language/genre, scoped goals | Style observations, confirmed preferences, provisional goals, voice profiles, reference-method analyses and practice/statistics records |
| Fiction | Author planning notes, versioned manuscript references, applicable setting versions | Material maps, structural alternatives, work-local settings, knowledge states, timelines, ambiguities, conflicts and impact records |
| Review | Versioned inputs permitted by the reading condition | Frozen reading responses, editorial reports and genre-sensitive three-perspective synthesis |
| State | Author choices, reasons, open points and owner references | Decision and handoff records, not a second manuscript/style/setting store |
| LibraryBridge | Formal shared-setting references and selected change proposals | Acquired version references, provenance, difference reports and prepared transfer packets |
| Optional external shared-setting provider | Selected formal shared-setting content | Formal versions only after a provider interface is implemented and configured |
| Sources / Reading / Evidence | External research actually acquired and read | Original research sources, extraction locators and real-world claim assessments |

Style can interpret how delayed disclosure affects rhythm; Fiction owns the actual disclosure plan and who knows what. Review evaluates those records under a particular reading condition. A structural interpretation is not a manuscript event or an author decision.

## Story development handoff

1. Begin with material the author elects to provide: events, people, relations, notes or text fragments. No complete manuscript is required; no real creative material is bundled.
2. Fiction prepares a material map with actual references, alternative interpretations and gaps. Prose remains in Writing; external research remains in Sources. Fiction may own original planning notes that are not manuscript text.
3. Explore different centers and single-line or multi-center plans. Each describes focus, selection/order, development, possible closure/open questions and proposed additions or transformations. Candidate count and presentation remain adjustable.
4. Compare gains, losses, missing links and dependencies. Retain alternatives as needed. Record author adoption in State before an alternative becomes the chosen working plan.
5. Writing owns prose revisions. Fiction and Style refer to returned versions; Review evaluates appropriate frozen inputs. Re-anchor moved passages rather than reusing obsolete chapter or paragraph locations.
6. After an author-confirmed significant change, identify impacts and review by priority batches. Preserve unread/unreviewed areas and distinguish artistic effects from demonstrated contradictions.

## Style handoff

The author selects samples. Style observes expression and composition at author/work/narrator/character levels with examples and counterexamples. An observed habit is not automatically a desired feature. Reference study explains methods and adaptations; translations retain edition/translator provenance and attribution limits.

Statistics supplement reading when scope, language, method and comparability are explicit. Templates record calculations actually performed; they do not calculate anything. Actual-work assistance and independent practice are optional and combinable. Writing owns practice prose; selection into a style sample set is a separate author choice.

## Review conditions and versioning

Use the [fiction review protocol](../../Review/current/fiction-review-protocol.md). First readers receive only a reading prefix and permissible instructions, excluding future events, author intent, setting spoilers and previous editorial reactions. Full editors may use selected full-context records. Preserve each first response before cross-review. Simulated perspectives are not real-reader statistics.

The existing Review CLI freezes Writing/Evidence inputs and supports a poem-only blind representation. It does not prepare progressive fiction contexts or freeze Fiction/Style records. A host performing such work must prepare explicit owner-local supplemental records with version/digest references and disclose unavailable context isolation. Templates alone do not establish that a review was performed.

## Shared-setting authority

A deliberately configured future provider may own formal shared-setting versions; Textura owns work-local settings, experiments and proposals. Each work adopts an explicit version. A new version prompts differences and impacts, not automatic adoption. The Bibliotheca connector is optional and disabled by default; no formal-setting write adapter is implemented.

See the [shared-setting interface](../../LibraryBridge/current/shared-setting-interface.md). Research remains limited reference and preservation selected. Library verification is not author acceptance of a fictional setting. Exact provider update commands and semantics remain unresolved implementation work.

## Readiness

Available in this candidate: shared capability descriptions, the registered Fiction protocol component, owner-local blank templates and continuation rules. No personal preset or private decision records are bundled.

Not added: new CLI record handlers, automatic extraction/impact/statistics engines, runtime privacy isolation, model execution, BM setting update endpoints or actual creative samples.
