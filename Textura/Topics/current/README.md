# Topics

Owns topics, research objects, works and series under `current/records/<type>/`. `topics.py` creates a record with an explicit parent topic; a topic can support many works and several series. Display titles are multilingual; generated IDs and directories are ASCII.

Every work initially has an independent language identity. Record translation/adaptation or cross-topic relationships explicitly with a reason and confirmation state; an AI-suggested connection is a proposal, not an author belief. Creating a work does not copy the topic's full research into every prompt. A local-only topic restricts its derived work.

Current CLI creates objects and works. Broader relationship editing is an agent-assisted record operation following Contracts and the versioning rules; it is not an automatic semantic search service. Drafts belong to Writing, claims to Evidence, research briefs to Research, and reusable library originals remain in Bibliotheca Magna where applicable.
