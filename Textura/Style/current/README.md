# Language, genre and personal style

Style owns author-selected sample catalogues, scoped style observations, confirmed preferences, provisional goals, reference-method studies and practice discussions under `current/records/`. It covers language choices and narrative or compositional methods, with optional author, work, narrator and character voice layers. Authors may use analysis of current works, independent practice, or both; neither mode has a fixed priority.

## Inputs and outputs

Inputs are explicitly selected Writing draft versions or Sources/Reading reference versions, actual passage locators, language and genre context, and the author's task and confirmed constraints. A selection may include experiments or collaborative drafts when their purpose and provenance are explicit. Selection for analysis does not make every observed feature a preference.

Outputs are grounded feature reports, scoped profiles, reference-method comparisons, optional documented statistics and practice or revision proposals. Preserve examples, counterexamples, limits and alternatives. Reference studies explain how a method works and possible ways to adapt it; admiration for another writer is not a personal rule.

## Ownership and collaboration

- Writing owns actual work, trial and practice text versions and candidate text edits. Style owns analysis, exercise briefs and links to those versions; do not create a competing manuscript archive here.
- Fiction owns characters, story events, knowledge states, alternatives and concrete structure plans. Style discusses how expression and narrative presentation work, referring to Fiction identities and plans rather than duplicating them.
- Review owns frozen review rounds and reading responses. Pass applicable criteria only when the reading condition permits them. First-time or blind readers must not receive withheld intentions, future plot information or explanatory style profiles through Style links.
- Sources and Reading own acquired reference materials and extractions. Translation owns translations and language uncertainty; Evidence owns external factual claim assessment.
- State records consequential author decisions and handoffs by reference. Author choices do not silently alter another component's records.

The shared core supports configurable authors and works. An author's provisional interests are not universal defaults or established traits without an appropriate basis. Shared fictional settings remain a Fiction/LibraryBridge concern; Style does not write to Bibliotheca Magna.
