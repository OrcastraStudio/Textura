# Feedback operating protocol

1. Receive the author-selected comment and identify the related work/version and available public context. Preserve the text and source/locator as actually available. Mark missing context.
2. Distinguish the reader's claim, question, interpretation, evidence lead, preference or apparent misunderstanding. Avoid inferring motives or private traits from a comment.
3. Interpret criticism fairly and consider whether the original work or published excerpt invited the reading. Agreement/disagreement counts are not evidence of truth.
4. Separate new empirical claims requiring Evidence assessment from interpretive disagreements that may remain open. Register cited sources when actually accessed, retaining what was read and what was merely mentioned.
5. Propose an appropriate outcome: no change, clarification, further research, a candidate revision, a possible reply or a new related work. Give reasons and preserve disagreement.
6. Link substantial new questions to Topics and accepted decisions to State. Preserve the publication/manuscript version the comment addressed; do not treat it as a review of an unseen later draft.
7. Prepare a reply only when useful or requested. Sending it is outside this initial module's scope. Selected feedback does not authorize broader collection or platform access.
