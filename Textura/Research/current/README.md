# Research

Owns research briefs, retrieval logs, questions and proposed connections under `current/records/`. Research uses Sources for registered originals and Evidence for claim assessments; it does not maintain a competing evidence ledger.

Execution: the current AI host performs retrieval and reasoning using available tools. This component's protocols and templates are not an autonomous crawler or installed search service.

Read [protocol.md](protocol.md), then copy [research-brief.template.md](research-brief.template.md) into an owner-local record when needed. Follow [Governance](../../Governance/current/AGENTS.md), [Contracts](../../Contracts/current/README.md) and current configuration.
