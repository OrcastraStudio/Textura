# Research operating protocol

1. Identify the topic, concrete questions, intended work and known source constraints. Separate exploratory questions from propositions already asserted in the manuscript.
2. Default to a small initial search. Search across relevant languages without a language whitelist; use variant names and specialist terminology when useful. Prefer sources capable of answering the question, not merely those easiest to read.
3. Log actual queries, access dates, source identities and what was retrieved. Distinguish a search snippet, abstract, secondary account and original full text. Do not fill metadata from inference.
4. If the optional Bibliotheca connection is deliberately configured and relevant to the task, use it for limited reference: explain the connection and date/context of prior material. Retrieve only relevant material; keep old views from narrowing the new question prematurely.
5. Offer a compact brief: possible angles, initial findings with source references, disagreements, missing evidence, a rough section suggestion if useful, and focused choices for the next step. Do not silently expand into a full research project.
6. When a deeper round is requested, record its scope and stopping point. Follow promising counterevidence as well as support, avoid treating repeated versions of one source as independent corroboration, and record limits of coverage.
7. Register actually used sources with Sources. Preserve obtainable originals/snapshots for quotations and key support, with honest capture completeness. Reading records extraction quality; Translation handles language uncertainty; Evidence judges support for specific claims.
8. Suggest connections proactively, explaining both why they matter and where the analogy may fail. Label these as proposals until accepted.
9. Preserve consequential findings and open questions in this component's records. Send decision summaries and links to State. Do not bulk ingest search results into Bibliotheca Magna.

Completion means the requested research scope is addressed with traceable results and stated gaps. It does not mean every claim is true or every possible source has been found.
