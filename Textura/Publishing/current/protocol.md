# Publication preparation protocol

1. Identify the author-selected full manuscript version, intended media, language and requested output. Finalization remains the author's choice after receiving checks and unresolved issues.
2. Default to a full work with selected excerpts, introductions or discussion questions. Create a substantially independent platform rewrite only when requested, and review its claims and argument separately.
3. Preserve the path from derivative back to full work and citations. Provide an actual access route if known; do not invent a public URL before publication. Mark a missing access route as pending.
4. Check that shortening has not removed a critical qualifier, turned speculation into fact, changed a quotation or hidden uncertainty. New claims introduced in promotional text need evidence too.
5. Adapt citation presentation to the medium while retaining complete source metadata and locators locally. Do not confuse the brevity of an excerpt with permission to lose traceability.
6. Check actual current platform constraints when preparing a concrete output. Do not hardcode remembered character limits, image ratios or upload requirements as permanent rules.
7. Record what was generated and what was actually checked, including the source manuscript version and unresolved issues. Rendered documents or images require the available relevant document/visual tools and inspection; a Markdown plan is not an exported PDF or Word file.
8. Deliver prepared artifacts to the author. Do not publish, schedule, send or log into a platform as an inferred next step. Later author-provided publication URLs may be recorded without changing the preserved publication package.

The full work and a later published derivative may diverge. Record that relationship; do not silently replace one with the other.

## Configurable starter presentation and correction defaults

- For prose and argumentative works, default to brief numbered references in the body with endnotes and a source list, allowing a work-specific override. Adapt the presentation to the medium without losing the internal claim/source/version/locator mapping. Poetry may use appropriate source or composition notes instead of forcing scholarly apparatus into the poem.
- For translated quotations, place the rendering in the work language in the body. Notes retain the corresponding original, source and translator or translation method; key original terms may appear inline. Coordinate with Translation and preserve uncertainty.
- Include a brief accurate AI-assistance statement in public works by default, normally at the end. Describe only actual contributions, such as research organization, language discussion, review or drafting. Complete prompts and conversations need not be published.
- Exploratory hypotheses may remain in the work with their status, evidence, counterexamples and gaps visible. Do not shorten a hypothesis into a factual assertion. For argumentative works, incorporate strong relevant objections naturally into the body or notes, respond where justified, and retain unanswered limitations. AI-created hypothetical objections are not attributed external opinions.
- Minor corrections that do not change meaning, such as spelling or formatting, need no separate public change note. Substantive changes to facts, quotations, reasoning or conclusions carry a date and concise explanation. Preserve previous versions and reasons in the owning Writing/Publishing records. Record the date actually known; a prepared note does not establish that a correction was published.
- Prepare corrections and their notes for author publication. This policy does not authorize direct modification of an external Word file, a public post or an immutable earlier publication package.
