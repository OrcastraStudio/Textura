# Analysis operating protocol

1. Determine whether the request is interpretation/checking of a published result or a new analysis. Record the author's scope for new work.
2. Identify datasets and exact versions, source locators, units, populations, dates, samples and missingness. Preserve originals and note access or processing restrictions.
3. State the question and planned comparisons before inspecting many alternatives where feasible. Record exploratory changes rather than presenting them as preplanned.
4. Keep cleaning, exclusions, recoding, unit conversion and joins explicit and reproducible. Never silently replace missing values or change denominators.
5. Use methods appropriate to the question and data. Record assumptions, parameter choices, software/tool versions and diagnostic limitations. Do not imply causal identification from correlation alone.
6. Retain calculations or scripts and their inputs alongside outputs under this owner's record directory. Graphs must identify units, sample/context and uncertainty where applicable; do not suppress inconvenient ranges or observations without explanation.
7. Distinguish reproduced published findings, failed reproductions and new observations. Do not attribute a Textura calculation to the original author.
8. Test numerical results against a simple independent check where practical. Inspect whether conclusions depend on a fragile choice or missing data. State what was and was not checked.
9. Send specific factual/statistical claims and supporting result locators to Evidence. The Review module can challenge methods and inferences using the frozen analysis references.

A successful calculation is not a successful argument by itself. Preserve null, ambiguous and contrary results, and avoid reporting more precision than the data support.
