# Community conduct draft

Proposed standards for discussion: critique ideas and text respectfully; avoid harassment, personal attacks and disclosure of another person's private writing or information. Describe disagreements with evidence and leave room for different languages, literary aims and experience levels.

The project owner still needs to adopt this policy and name a reporting/enforcement route. This draft does not claim an appointed moderation team, response-time guarantee or a private channel that does not exist. Do not post sensitive reports publicly.

This short project-specific draft is not represented as an adopted third-party code of conduct.
